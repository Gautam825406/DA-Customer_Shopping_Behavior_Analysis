## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **customer shopping behavior** using transactional retail data and presents **actionable insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard**.

The objective is to help businesses understand:
- Customer preferences  
- Purchasing patterns  
- Revenue drivers  
- Subscription behavior  

The project follows an **end-to-end data analytics workflow** using **Python, SQL (PostgreSQL), and Power BI** — from raw data processing to business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
A retail company aims to:

- Understand changing customer purchase patterns  
- Identify high-value customer segments  
- Evaluate the impact of subscriptions, discounts, and shipping types  
- Improve customer retention and revenue  

### 🔑 Key Business Question
> **How can customer shopping data be leveraged to improve sales, engagement, and customer loyalty?**

---

## 🧩 Dataset Summary
- **Total Records:** 3,900 transactions  
- **Total Columns:** 18  

### 🔍 Key Features
- **Customer Demographics:** Age, Gender, Location  
- **Purchase Details:** Category, Item Purchased, Purchase Amount  
- **Customer Behavior:** Subscription Status, Discount Applied, Review Rating  
- **Logistics & Payments:** Shipping Type, Payment Method

## 🔍 Data Preparation & Feature Engineering (Python)
- Data cleaning and validation  
- Handling missing values in **Review Rating**  
- Standardizing column names  
- Feature engineering:
  - `age_group`
  - `purchase_frequency_days`
- Removing redundant columns  
- Exporting cleaned data to **PostgreSQL**

---

## 🗄️ SQL Analysis (PostgreSQL)
Key business questions answered:

- Revenue comparison by gender  
- High-spending customers who used discounts  
- Top 5 products by average review rating  
- Purchase behavior by shipping type  
- Subscribers vs non-subscribers comparison  
- Discount-dependent products  
- Customer segmentation (**New, Returning, Loyal**)  
- Top products by category  
- Subscription likelihood of repeat buyers

## 📌 Key Insights
- 📈 **Subscribers generate higher total revenue**
- 👕 **Clothing & Accessories** are top-performing categories
- 👥 **Young Adults & Middle-Aged customers** drive maximum revenue
- 💸 Discounts boost sales but need margin control
- 🔁 **Loyal customers** form the largest segment

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Promote **subscription benefits** to increase retention  
- Introduce **loyalty programs** for repeat buyers  
- Optimize **discount strategies** to protect profit margins  
- Focus marketing on **high-revenue age groups**  
- Highlight **top-rated and best-selling products**
